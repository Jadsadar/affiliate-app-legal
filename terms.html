Affiliate App (the "App") · Last updated: 2026-10-17

1. What the App is

The App is a personal desktop tool operated by its owner. It uploads the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts using TikTok's official Content Posting API (upload flow). The App does not publish anything automatically: the owner reviews each draft in the TikTok app and posts it manually.

2. Who may use it

The App is intended only for its owner, who connects their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and does not provide any service to other TikTok users.

3. Acceptable use

The App must be used in line with TikTok's Terms of Service, Developer Terms and Community Guidelines. It must not be used to scrape TikTok, to post content the owner has no right to use, or to post spam or misleading content.

6. Ending access

Access can be ended at any time by removing the App's permission in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) and deleting the locally stored token.
