Privacy Policy

Affiliate App (the "App") · Last updated: 2026-10-17

1. Summary

The App is a personal tool used by its owner on their own computer. It does not run a server, has no other users, and does not collect or share personal data about anyone else.

2. Data the App handles

  • TikTok authorization data for the owner's own account: an access token, a refresh token and the account's open ID, received through TikTok Login Kit after the owner approves access.
  • The owner's own video files, which are uploaded to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts.

2.1 Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer. They are never sent to any server other than TikTok's official API endpoints, and are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

3. How the data is used

Only to authenticate with TikTok and to upload the owner's own videos as inbox drafts. The App does not read the owner's followers, messages, or other users' data.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the App. The owner can delete them at any time by running the disconnect command (which removes the local token file) and by removing the App's permission in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).

5. Third parties

The App communicates only with TikTok's official APIs. TikTok's own Privacy Policy applies to data handled by TikTok.
